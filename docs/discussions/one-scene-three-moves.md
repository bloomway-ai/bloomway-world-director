# One scene. Three camera moves. What would you shoot?

**EN · 中文**

Use one OSS example (`examples/edo-rainy-night`, `modern-city-street`, or `mars-base`) or your own `plan.json` on the **same blocking**. Design **three** distinct camera moves we already support in Studio:

1. **Push in** — move closer on the subject  
2. **Pull back** — reveal layout / context  
3. **Follow or orbit** — track the capsule or arc around the action  

## How to participate

1. Fork or run locally (`npm run dev`).  
2. Export or paste your `camera.keys` (or full `plan.json`).  
3. Post a screenshot + short note on **why** each move serves the beat.  
4. No prizes, no compute credits — practice for previs thinking only.
