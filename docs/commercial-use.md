## What the MIT License covers

The standard MIT License in the root `LICENSE` file applies to **source code and documentation that are explicitly part of this open-source repository** and distributed under that license.

It does **not** automatically cover:

| Category | Status |
| -------- | ------ |
| Bloomway hosted services, APIs, and MCP backends | Separate service terms on [bloomway.ai](https://bloomway.ai) |
| Paid model APIs, cloud compute, or third-party subscriptions you configure | Your agreements with those providers |
| Example media, fonts, or assets marked with their own license files | See [THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md) and per-example README files |
| Unreleased or proprietary Bloomway platform modules | Not distributed here |

Using this project **locally or on your own infrastructure** under the MIT License does **not** require registration, email notification, separate permission, or revenue sharing on works you create.

We do **not** charge a “commercial license fee” for running the MIT-licensed code yourself.

## What may cost money (independent of MIT)

Bloomway may charge for **services**, such as:

- Hosted previs, generation, storage, or collaboration on bloomway.ai
- Deployment assistance, training, custom development, or support agreements
- Official partnership or certification programs

Those are optional and separate from exercising your rights under the MIT License.

## Contact (voluntary)

**Commercial use does not require contacting us.**

We welcome you to reach out at **info@bloomway.ai** if you want to:

- Share a commercial or creative project built with Bloomway World Director
- Discuss collaboration or co-development
- Request deployment support or custom development
- Explore official partnership or certification

With your permission, we may feature your project in a community showcase.

Bloomway AI also welcomes more developers and filmmaking enthusiasts to build together—use the same email if you would like to contribute ideas, integrations, or case studies.
